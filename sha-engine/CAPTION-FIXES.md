# Three published captions to fix by hand

Instagram's API cannot edit a published caption — Meta exposes no endpoint for
it, and Composio's own guidance confirms the only API "fix" is posting a
corrective comment, which would leave the broken text in place and read badly.

Captions **are** editable manually. On each post: tap `⋯` → **Edit** → replace
the caption → tick.

All three replacements were checked against the Critic: no bare hashtags, no
drafting artifacts, within the 4–8 hashtag band and 2-emoji cap from
`config/brand.json`.

## Why only three

Twelve of the last twelve posts carry stripped hashtags. These three are the
ones worth her time: the newest (the fault is ongoing), the best-performing
(most eyes on the defect), and the one with two visible placeholders.

The rest are older and their reach has already passed. Fixing them recovers
little. The Critic now blocks all of it going forward — `bare-hashtag` and
`draft-artifact` are both blockers, and no post reaches the queue carrying them.
